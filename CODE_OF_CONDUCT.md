# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Offgrid to be a welcoming project for everyone who uses it, reports a bug, or sends a fix,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback about the work, not the person
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use Offgrid

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will
explain why when it is appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, such as the repository, its issues, pull
requests, and discussions, and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to [pacsybusiness@gmail.com](mailto:pacsybusiness@gmail.com). Every
report will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## Consequences

Depending on how serious the behavior is, the maintainers may respond with:

1. **A private warning** explaining what was wrong and why.
2. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
3. **A permanent ban** for repeated or severe violations, such as harassment.
